Subtitled Privacy Policy

Subtitled · Last updated 12 November 2026

Subtitled is a Mac app that shows live subtitles for meetings, videos and anything else you hear on your Mac in another language. It is published on the Mac App Store by Sanjay Keyan ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

In short: everything happens on your Mac. Speech recognition and translation run on your Mac with Apple's built-in technology. Subtitled has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no tracking, and the App Store version has no permission to connect to the internet itself. Audio and subtitles are never sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing is saved unless you choose to save a transcript. We receive no personal information about you.

No account, no sign-up

You never create an account or give us a name, email address or password. We keep no profile of you.

The sound you choose to subtitle

You choose one app (for example Microsoft Teams, Zoom or a web browser), and Subtitled listens only to the sound from that app while you are translating. To do this, macOS asks you to allow Screen & System Audio Recording. Subtitled uses only the sound from the app you choose; it does not look at, record or save your screen. While it listens, the menu bar icon is filled and the subtitles show a LIVE marker.

The speech is turned into text and translated into the language you choose on your Mac, using Apple's on-device speech recognition and translation. macOS may ask you to allow Speech Recognition; with Subtitled, speech is still recognised on your Mac. The first time you use a language, macOS downloads Apple's language models from Apple. That download contains no audio or text from you.

Subtitles and transcripts

Subtitles are shown on screen and kept in your Mac's memory while the app is open, so that you can save them if you want to. They are cleared when you start a new session or quit. Nothing is written to disk unless you choose Save Transcript…, which saves a file where you choose; that file is yours and stays on your Mac unless you share it.

Your settings and vocabulary

Your settings, including the vocabulary list of names and terms you add, are stored in the app's preferences on your Mac. They are never sent anywhere.

Free trial and purchase

Subtitled is free to download, with a free 2-Day Trial and a one-time Subtitled Lifetime purchase. There is no subscription. Apple handles the trial and the payment through the App Store. We never receive your card details, billing address or Apple Account details; the app only learns from Apple whether the trial or Lifetime is active, and when the trial started.

The trial lasts 2 days or 15 minutes of translation, whichever comes first. To count the minutes, Subtitled keeps the number of minutes used, and the latest date it has seen, on your Mac: in its preferences and in your Mac's keychain (an item named "Subtitled free trial"). They are not sent anywhere.

What we do not use

Subtitled contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, attribution or social-media software, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in macOS settings, Apple passes them on without identifying you.

The people you subtitle

Subtitled shows what other people say. Please let the people in a meeting know you are using live subtitles, and follow your organisation's rules and the law where you are. Their speech, like yours, stays on your Mac.

Children

Subtitled is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

  • Subtitles: start a new session or quit the app. Delete any transcript file you saved as you would any other file.
  • Vocabulary and settings: clear the vocabulary in Subtitled's Settings, or delete the app, which removes its preferences.
  • Trial count: the keychain item "Subtitled free trial" holds only a number of minutes and a date. You can delete it in the Keychain Access app.

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export on our side. If you have a question about your data or your rights, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date. A change that affects what information leaves your Mac will be made before the version of the app that needs it is released.

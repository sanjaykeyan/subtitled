Subtitled Support

Help with Subtitled, live subtitles for meetings and videos on your Mac.

Write to us: sanjaykeyan.developer@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days. Please include your macOS version, the meeting app you listen to and the language.

How do I start?

Click the captions icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen. Under Listen to, choose the app with the sound (Teams, Zoom, a browser…), choose the language people speak under Meeting language and yours under Subtitles in, then press Start Translating. Subtitles appear in a window you can drag, resize or minimise.

Subtitled can't hear the meeting.

Subtitled needs Screen & System Audio Recording. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Subtitled, then quit and reopen Subtitled. It uses only the sound from the app you choose. Also check that the app you chose under Listen to is the one playing the sound (for Teams on the web, choose your browser).

The subtitles are in the original language, not the one I chose.

The translation languages need a one-time download. In the menu bar panel, press Download on the card that asks for it, or open System Settings, then General, then Language & Region, then Translation Languages.

Which languages work?

The meeting can be in Chinese, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese or Spanish. Subtitles can be in English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese or Spanish (choose under Subtitles in). The first session in a new language downloads its speech model once, which can take a minute.

Names and terms come out wrong.

Add them to the Vocabulary list (in the panel, or Settings > Vocabulary), one per line: people, clients, products. To force a translation, write it as TRI = IRR.

The subtitles are in the way.

Drag them anywhere, resize them from the edges, or press – to shrink them to a small LIVE marker; click it to bring them back. The text size is in the panel.

How does the free trial work?

The free 2-Day Trial lasts 2 days or 15 minutes of translation, whichever comes first. After that, translating needs Subtitled Lifetime, a one-time purchase with no subscription. If the trial ends during a session, the session carries on for up to an hour so the meeting isn't cut off.

I bought Lifetime, but Subtitled asks me to unlock it.

In the menu bar panel, choose ⋯ > Unlock Subtitled…, then Restore Purchases, while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, or see Apple's guide, Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Does Subtitled record or upload meetings?

No. Speech recognition and translation run on your Mac. Nothing is saved unless you choose Save Transcript…, and nothing is sent to us. Please let the people in the meeting know you're using live subtitles.

What do I need?

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
